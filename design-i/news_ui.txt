# News, Announcements & Economic Calendar UI Textual Description

## 1. Overall Philosophy & Aesthetics

*   **Consistency:** Uphold the modern, clean, intuitive, and premium design language used across the application.
*   **Readability:** Prioritize ease of reading and information absorption, especially for text-heavy content.
*   **Organization:** Structure information logically so users can quickly find what they need.
*   **Typography & Colors:** Consistent with the overall application design (Lato/Open Sans/Noto Sans, defined light/dark themes). Use semantic colors sparingly, primarily for status or importance indicators.

## 2. Main News Feed Page (Dedicated "News" Section)

*   **Navigation:** Typically accessed via the main application navigation sidebar/top bar.
*   **Page Title:** Prominent title, e.g., "Market News & Analysis."
*   **Layout Options (User-selectable, with a default):**
    *   **List View (Default):**
        *   Each news item is a row.
        *   Columns/Elements per row:
            *   **Read/Unread Indicator:** Small dot or subtle background change.
            *   **Headline:** Bold, primary focus. Clickable to open Article View.
            *   **Source:** Name of the news provider (e.g., "Xinhua Finance," "Caixin").
            *   **Timestamp:** How long ago it was published (e.g., "2h ago," "Yesterday," or specific date).
            *   **Brief Snippet:** 1-2 lines of the article's beginning to provide context.
            *   **Tags/Categories (Optional):** Small tags indicating relevance (e.g., "Tech Sector," "Earnings Report").
            *   **Thumbnail Image (Optional):** If available from the source, a small image aligned with the text.
    *   **Card View:**
        *   Each news item is a card.
        *   Cards arranged in a grid or masonry layout.
        *   Elements per card:
            *   **Main Image (if available):** Prominently displayed at the top of the card.
            *   **Headline:** Below the image.
            *   **Snippet:** Shorter than in list view.
            *   **Source & Timestamp:** Subtly at the bottom of the card.
            *   **Read/Unread Indicator.**
*   **Filtering and Sorting Controls:**
    *   **Location:** A dedicated filter bar/panel, typically at the top of the news feed or in a collapsible sidebar.
    *   **Filter by Category:** Dropdown or tabs. Examples:
        *   "All News"
        *   "General Market"
        *   "Sector News" (further drill-down by sector if possible)
        *   "My Watchlist News" (news related to stocks in user's watchlists)
        *   "My Portfolio News" (news related to stocks in user's portfolios)
    *   **Filter by Source (If multiple distinct providers integrated):** Checkboxes or multi-select dropdown.
    *   **Sort By:** Dropdown. Options:
        *   "Latest First" (default)
        *   "Oldest First"
        *   "Relevance" (if search is active)
*   **Search Functionality:**
    *   **Search Bar:** Prominently placed within the News section.
    *   **Scope:** Searches within headlines and article content.
    *   **Results:** Updates the feed to show matching articles.
*   **Pagination/Infinite Scroll:** For navigating through a large number of articles.
*   **"Mark All as Read" Button:** Useful for users who want to clear unread indicators.

## 3. Article View

*   **Access:** Clicking a headline from the Main News Feed Page. Can open in a modal (for quick reads), a new page, or a master-detail view. A dedicated page is often better for focused reading.
*   **Layout:** Single-column, focused on the article content. Ample margins.
*   **Content Elements:**
    *   **Headline:** Large and clear at the top.
    *   **Source & Author:** Displayed below the headline (e.g., "Source: Reuters / Author: John Doe").
    *   **Publication Date & Time:** Clearly visible.
    *   **Main Article Body:**
        *   Clean, highly legible font (consistent with app's body font).
        *   Appropriate line height and paragraph spacing.
        *   Images or videos embedded within the content should be responsive.
    *   **Tags/Keywords:** Displayed at the end of the article, clickable to find related news.
*   **Readability Features:**
    *   **Font Size Adjustment:** Controls (e.g., A-/A+ buttons) to increase/decrease font size.
    *   **Theme Consistency:** Adheres to the user's selected light/dark theme.
*   **Related Articles Section:**
    *   Displayed below the main article content or in a sidebar.
    *   Lists 3-5 headlines of articles on similar topics or related to the same stock/sector.
*   **Social Sharing Options (Optional):**
    *   Subtle icons for sharing on popular platforms (e.g., WeChat, Weibo, Twitter, LinkedIn).
*   **Navigation:** Clear "Back" button to return to the news feed. Breadcrumbs if the view is deep.

## 4. Company Announcements Section

*   **Access:** Typically a dedicated tab within the "News" section or a sub-section under a stock's detailed view.
*   **Page Title:** e.g., "Company Announcements" or "Listed Company Filings."
*   **Layout:** Tabular list, prioritizing clarity and quick access to documents.
*   **Search/Filter:**
    *   **Search by Stock Code/Company Name:** Primary way to find specific announcements.
    *   **Filter by Announcement Type (Optional):** Dropdown (e.g., "Financial Reports," "Shareholder Meetings," "Suspension/Resumption").
    *   **Filter by Date Range.**
*   **Table Columns:**
    *   **Date:** Publication date of the announcement.
    *   **Time:** Publication time.
    *   **Stock Code:**
    *   **Stock Name:**
    *   **Announcement Title/Headline:** Official title of the filing.
    *   **Link/Source:** Direct link to the PDF document or the official source page (e.g., exchange website). An icon indicating it's a PDF link.
*   **Sorting:** Sortable by date (default newest first), stock code.
*   **Pagination:** For navigating through numerous announcements.

## 5. Economic Calendar Section

*   **Access:** Typically a dedicated tab within the "News" section or its own top-level item if deemed important enough.
*   **Page Title:** e.g., "Economic Calendar."
*   **Layout:** Tabular list of economic events.
*   **Date Navigation:**
    *   Quick filters for "Today," "Tomorrow," "This Week," "Next Week."
    *   A calendar control to select specific dates or date ranges.
*   **Table Columns:**
    *   **Date:** Date of the event.
    *   **Time:** Time of the event (clearly specify timezone, or convert to user's local time).
    *   **Country/Region:** Flag icon and name (e.g., "China," "USA").
    *   **Event Name:** Description of the economic data point (e.g., "GDP Growth Rate YoY," "Manufacturing PMI," "CPI MoM").
    *   **Impact:** Visual indicator of expected market impact (e.g., Low, Medium, High, often represented by color-coded icons like 1 to 3 bull heads or similar).
    *   **Actual:** The reported value (updated after release).
    *   **Forecast:** The consensus forecast value.
    *   **Previous:** The value from the previous period.
*   **Filtering:**
    *   **By Country/Region:** Select multiple countries.
    *   **By Impact Level:** Select impact levels to display.
    *   **By Category (Optional):** E.g., Inflation, Employment, GDP, Central Bank.
*   **Event Details (Optional):** Clicking an event might expand to show a brief explanation of the indicator or a historical chart.

## 6. UI Aesthetics & Common Elements

*   **Whitespace:** Generous use to ensure content is not cramped and is easy to scan.
*   **Typography Hierarchy:** Clear distinction between headlines, sub-headlines, body text, and metadata (source, date).
*   **Icons:** Consistent icon set for actions (e.g., search, filter, external link, PDF), categories, and impact levels.
*   **Loading States:** Visual feedback (spinners, placeholders) when data is being fetched.
*   **Empty States:** Informative messages if no news/announcements/events match filters or for initial loading.
*   **Responsiveness:** All sections must be responsive and usable on various screen sizes.

This structure aims to provide users with comprehensive and easily digestible information regarding market news, official announcements, and key economic indicators.
